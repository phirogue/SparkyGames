# Novel Revision — Third Edition Spec

Source: four fresh in-persona reviews (mystery reader / fantasy reader /
experienced writer / young reader), 2026-08-14, run blind against the current
text with no access to prior reviews or drafts. This file records (1) which
findings were accepted, modified, or rejected for THIS project, and (2) the
exact change list per chapter. Rewrite agents execute section 3 verbatim;
where a description of the current text is slightly off, honor the intent.

Consensus verdicts for the record: mystery 4/5 ("best piece of mystery
engineering I've read this year" for the mirror-stitch), fantasy 4/5,
writer "strong late second draft," young reader 8.5/10. Nobody asked for a
teardown; everybody asked for the same handful of surgical cuts. This
edition is therefore surgery, not demolition.

## 1. Adjudication

Project context that governs these calls: the novel shares the game's canon
(four strengths, Moonlight naming, "spent is spent", Hollow Court, Court of
Whiskers, AI-honesty page) and the owner's standing decisions (every choice
needs a consequence; repetition is the enemy). Canon facts and the previous
edition's unanimous untouchables (section 2) bind this round too.

### ACCEPTED

1. **Bodkin's currency has no stated source** (fantasy critic's "hole below
   the waterline"; mystery critic #9 concurs). The strongest new finding: in
   a book whose religion is balanced books, the villain's wound-down-life
   spending never reconciles against his one life. Fix: one paragraph at the
   Gentleman's (ch. 6) pricing the stock — stolen half-seconds wound and
   banked, and under them an advance of credit from the thing that holds
   lives in escrow. Closes the loop and sharpens the fraud theme.
2. **The two prior stitcher murders never get a motive** (mystery #1). The
   confession says "put it all on the bill" and the bill omits two murders.
   Fix: on the flats, the bill is read ENTIRE (see also item 4) — the
   stitchers were the only hands that could have re-anchored the seam,
   removed decades ahead, the way a professional removes locks.
3. **The prologue's death-classification bends once, unexplained** (fantasy
   #2) + **the parlor question is never asked** (mystery #7) + **the
   long-way-home guilt is never adjudicated** (writer 7a). One cluster, one
   fix, in two rooms: (a) ch. 9 thread-audit — the Clerk notes the first
   appointment was posted by no hand of the Court; (b) ch. 12 flats —
   Bodkin owns it: he posted the appointment, the gentlest slot on the
   ledger, because the kit was going to spend that life regardless; and the
   eleven-minute route bought nothing — the work was done between the
   boiling and the scream. The book's most careful reader gets receipts;
   Ash gets the absolution the note couldn't give.
4. **Bodkin's moral bill is settled at a discount** (mystery, THE UGLY). The
   auditor never totals what his mentor did to a city. Fix: settlements
   state accounts — on the flats, after "Put it all on the bill," Ash reads
   the full bill aloud, formally, before any payment. Grief does not get to
   lose a page.
5. **The climax argues law when it should argue love** (writer #2) + the
   slack-evidence contradiction (Bodkin has held the slack in his own teeth
   all season). Fix: plant the slack observation in ch. 5 (the warehouse
   re-sewing shows lifted stitches release as dead slack; Ash says it aloud
   on the sea-wall), compress the fraud-law paragraph at the flats, and add
   the clause that turns contradiction into character: he has been carrying
   the proof for months and not looking at it. The scene then turns once,
   on the kettle.
6. **The nine-lives motivation hole** (writer #3): why could none of the
   nine new-made lives be wound into Bodkin? One clause of law in the
   confession: winding wants an unspent spool; a body spent from is stitch
   to the heart and takes no new thread. Eleven words convert avoidable
   tragedy into tragedy.
7. **Mid-book invincibility** (writer #1, young #5, mystery "Ash stops
   losing"). Fixes, all cheap: (a) the CLERICAL ERROR moment goes ON CAMERA
   in ch. 6's cold REFUSED scene — the twist's best clue-track finally gets
   a dramatized instance; (b) ch. 10 gains one honest failure: a ward Ash
   mended fails, and he mends it again, uglier, and it holds; (c) ch. 9's
   third identical resource-itemization becomes sensation (also writer 7c —
   the character sheet stops showing).
8. **The epilogue closes every account except the one that matters** (writer
   #6; fantasy #6; mystery #4). Fixes: (a) one compact paragraph returning
   the blank page and the taken Remembered Day — the estate's one entry
   held open on purpose; (b) the unclaimed oilskin explicitly charged to
   the contested weeks, per Ash's own "charge that to me"; (c) Sootbeard
   and Shear paragraphs trimmed to hold net length. Everything from "And
   the house on Needle Lane is warm." remains untouchable.
9. **Aphorism saturation** (all four; the writer counted: 68 "the way X"
   similes, 40 "which is" pivots, 27 "professional(ly)", ~30 forward-flash
   interjections). Fix is a ration, not a sanding — see Voice rules G6–G9.
   Transit and procedural passages go plain; scene-enders keep stingers;
   the calibration reference is the prologue's back half and ch. 12.
10. **Over-determination of the killer's identity** (mystery #2,
    accept-modified). Sootbeard no longer says "shaped as a cat"; he files
    parameters and declines to conclude, and Ash supplies the evasion
    unprompted — the dodge beat survives, the loud early answer does not.
    (The dream stays where it is; see REJECTED.)
11. **Bell-anchor logic lands a chapter late** (fantasy #5). One Clerk
    sentence in the ch. 9 vault scene: it rang the hours, and the hours
    kept the appointments.
12. **The strolling gentleman is never booked** (fantasy #7, writer 7b).
    One clause in ch. 11's brokerage trail: the house walks behind its
    blind paper; the Grey Boss's unbookable gentleman was the house,
    attending its own commission.
13. **Caulker's shadow lacks unique cargo** (writer): it now establishes
    held-not-spent on the page — the wraith is crisp, not fraying, because
    its man is held whole — giving ch. 9's negotiation and ch. 12's
    contract talk a verified floor.
14. **Senior-grey herring needs one live beat** (mystery #6): two sentences
    at the hearing, Ash reading the old tom's gait on the gallery rail and
    hating himself for it, before the session-rolls clear him.
15. **Vocabulary landmines** (young): "fee simple" cut (plain words),
    "sold for hardcore" glossed, the moth's Latin given a plain-words key,
    the Hush glossed at first use (ch. 6), the parcel's 40/30-year
    arithmetic made explicit in one clause.
16. **Shear's herring is born and shot in the same scene** (writer 7d,
    light): delay the seller-inference from ch. 8 to the hearing; keep her
    wanting.

### MODIFIED

17. **"Dramatize one of the five offscreen refusals"** (writer #1). No new
    death scene: ch. 6's existing cold refusal absorbs the CLERICAL ERROR
    beat, so the count ledger stays exactly as printed.
18. **"Compress chapter 4" / "split it" / "move the Fish-Giver"** (all
    four, in conflict). Ruling per the prior edition's owner-context call:
    all four episodes stay (canon + epilogue plants), the chapter loses
    15–20% — Ratsmeet protocol preamble, the purr-case middle nights, a
    quarter of the Fish-Giver — and the "of whom more in later chapters"
    IOU goes.
19. **"The ending refunds everything / keep the dread"** (fantasy). The
    foreclosure joke stays (two other critics loved it; joke-then-knife is
    the book's signature); the *bill* becomes real instead (items 4, 8).

### REJECTED — does not fit this project

20. **A Bodkin POV chapter** (young #4). The book is a case file; the file
    cannot contain what Ash did not witness or receive in evidence.
    Breaking the device once breaks it everywhere. The secondhand "You'll
    take tea, then" is the point.
21. **Tansy at the flats** (young, again). Prior unanimous ruling stands:
    the Mere wants witnesses, not passengers; "Mind the house, Tansy" is
    now the book's generational motif and the young critic's own favorite
    payoff. She stays benched, and she stays furious about it.
22. **Move the eight-lights dream to ch. 11** (mystery #3). Re-plots a
    third of the book and unmotivates chs. 9–10 (the sea-wall line-drawing
    and the flats visit both run on it). The guessability complaint is
    treated by item 10 instead.
23. **Let Ash pay the anchor / "hero is a courier"** (writer, THE UGLY #3).
    The refusal of Ash's seven lives IS the book's law working: spent is
    spent, and Elspeth's charge — spend nothing you cannot re-sew — is the
    estate's one standing order. His payment is the Day, the blank page,
    and the bill he alone can state (item 4 gives him that agency on the
    stone). Restructuring the sacrifice would break canon and the book's
    own iron rule to satisfy a convention the book is deliberately
    declining.
24. **Chapter-ending lines go plain** (writer, partial). Prior unanimous
    untouchable: every existing chapter-ending line is preserved exactly.
25. **A map and glossary** (young #7). Production front-matter, not prose;
    noted for a future edition, out of scope here.
26. **Restructure the seated-authority confessions** (writer, THE BAD).
    Re-plots three chapters; registers were differentiated last edition;
    the ration (item 9) is this edition's answer. Logged honestly as
    known-and-deferred.

## 3. Change list per chapter

Global voice rules (every agent, in its own file only):
- **G6.** Thin "the way X does Y" similes by roughly a third; cut in transit
  and procedural paragraphs first; keep every instance carrying theme

- **G10.** Chapter-ending lines preserved EXACTLY. Plants and untouchables

- **G11.** LaTeX conventions: `---` em-dashes, `` ` `` ``/`''` quotes,
  `\emph{}`, `\scenebreak`; no new packages or macros. Edit surgically;
  do not re-typeset unchanged paragraphs.

## 4. Voice guardrails (carried forward, plus this edition's)

- Ash: first person, dry, precise, fond; epigrams permitted, no longer
  mandatory; in the scenes marked plain (E6-2, E9-3, E10-1, E12 all,
  E13-1) prefer declarative sentences and let silence work.
- Metaphors cash out to a concrete meaning. Referents resolve in-sentence.
- No mechanics exposition in narrative voice; no future-telling beyond the
  rationed flashes; narration reports what happened.
- New prose must be indistinguishable from the book's best pages (the last
  Remembered Day, the tea on the flats, the window).
