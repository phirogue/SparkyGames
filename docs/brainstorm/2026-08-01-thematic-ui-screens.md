# Thematic UI screens — how would a cat use a menu?

Owner prompt (2026-08-01): "brainstorm more ideas for the UI screens to make
them thematic, like how would a cat keep a journal?" Principle for all of
these: **Ash cannot read, write, or shop — but Elspeth could, and Ash owns
her things now.** Every screen is either an object of hers he has claimed,
or a pure cat behavior given interface duty. Ideas are art-cheap first
(programmatic animation over new paintings, per the effects rule).

## The Casebook (journal) — a dead witch's ledger, continued by paw

- The book is **Elspeth's case ledger**: her neat entries fill the front
  pages (backstory for free — flip back to read her old cases as lore).
- Ash "writes" the only way a cat can: **inked pawprint stamps**. Each deed
  is her handwriting style replaced by a paw stamp + a pasted keepsake — a
  whisker taped to the page, a pressed goose feather, a smear of wax.
- Choices the player made get a **red thread underline** — thread from her
  sewing box, one strand per decision, stitched right through the page.
- Corners of well-used pages carry **claw-sharpening scratches**; the more
  a page is revisited, the more shredded its corner (visit counter, free).
- Deaths are recorded by the book itself — her enchantment on the ledger
  writes the ninth-life entries in HER hand. The book knows. (Peak-end:
  every death entry ends on something she'd have said.)

## Knowledge / glossary — a scent index, not an encyclopedia

- Entries surface as **scent trails**: a new fact fades in as a colored
  wisp curling off the object's sketch (already have humour colors).
- Unknown entries aren't "???" — they're **things Ash hasn't smelled yet**:
  a blank silhouette with a nose-print icon.

## Quest board — the Mantel, and things knocked off it

- Quests are **objects on the mantelpiece**: a key, a sealed letter, a
  bell. Accepting a quest = **batting it off the shelf** (it tips, wobbles,
  falls with a satisfying clatter — pure programmatic animation).
- Completed quests return to the mantel as trophies, slightly chipped.

## Magpie Exchange (shop) — a nest, not a counter

- Inventory sits in a **magpie's nest of shiny things**; prices in gleam.
- The magpie **haggles by tilting its head**; refusing twice makes it hide
  the item under a wing (comes back next visit — no real scarcity).

## Loadout — the sewing table (mock exists: mock_loadout.png)

- Skills threaded through a **needle row**; energy stacks by color spool.
- Confirming = **biting the thread** — snip animation, kit is stitched.

## Achievements — scratches on the doorframe (mock exists: mock_achievements.png)

- Each achievement is a **scratch mark at a height** on the parlor doorframe
  — the cat measures itself the way children are measured. Rare ones gleam
  faintly of moonlight thread.

## Smaller screens

- **Pause**: Ash sits and grooms; the world blurs. Resume = ear flick.
- **Settings**: the cat-flap door — bells of three sizes for volume, a
  sunbeam slider for brightness, a collar toggle for notifications.
- **Chapter select**: the case-file drawer (mock exists in prompts doc).
- **Loading**: a red thread dot skitters; Ash's eyes track it. On slow
  loads he pounces and misses.
- **Lives/death**: **nine candles** on the windowsill; a life lost is a
  candle out, smoke curling into the shape of the mistake.
- **Map**: rooftops at night; visited places carry a **cheek-rub smudge**
  (cats mark territory by rubbing); quest targets glint like eyes.
