# AI Transparency — DECIDED (2026-08-02)

Owner decision: this game's development relies heavily on AI, and we are
plainly honest about it. It is **not a selling point** and **not a secret**.

## The policy

- **Honest when asked.** Any direct question — from a player, a journalist,
  or a store reviewer — gets a straight answer.
- **Disclosed in the credits and in store-listing fine print.** A plain
  statement, not buried, not shouted.
- **Never claim hand-made.** No "hand-drawn", "hand-painted", or
  "hand-crafted" language for anything AI-generated, in any copy, anywhere.
- **Never lead marketing with AI.** The pitch is the story, the cat, and the
  design — AI is a footnote, not a headline, in either direction.

## What is AI, what is human

| AI-generated / AI-assisted | Human |
|---|---|
| Art: stills via **ChatGPT**, motion via **Kling** | Story direction and writing decisions |
| Music: AI-generated (commercial-tier tools) | Game design decisions |
| Code: AI-assisted development | Taste, curation, and selection of every asset |
| | Final approval of everything that ships |

## The rule for copy

**All marketing copy — store listings, press kit, social posts, trailer
text — must be checked against this policy before it ships.** If a line
implies human authorship of an AI-generated asset, it doesn't ship.
