# Case File I — "Wax & Wick"

Chapter 1. The free chapter — it must be a complete, satisfying mystery on
its own ([monetization.md](../monetization.md)): a suspect pursued, a guild
exposed, a verdict chosen — and the *real* case cracked open on the way out.

**District:** Wickrow (Chandlers' quarter) + the Shambles (market, unlocked
mid-chapter). **Suspect:** Guildmaster **Ellery Wick**, master of the
Chandlers' Guild. **The question:** Elspeth died the week the Chandlers
bought all her debts. Cats do not believe in coincidence; coincidence is
what mice call carelessness.

**Chapter arc in one line:** Wick is guilty — of almost everything except
the murder. Proving that is how Ash learns what the murder actually was.

**Volume:** 5 main leads (9–11 prowls) + 5 side quests (6–8 prowls) + 1
flashback ≈ 1.5–2 hours. New mechanics introduced: Gleam & the Magpie
Exchange, Press On (push-your-luck), loadout editing, favor-knots, catnap
flashbacks.

## Main leads

### L1 — "The Smell of Money" *(1 prowl · hack & slash · teaches Press On)*

**Board card:** "Beeswax at the parlor window. Cheap tallow burns in
Thimblefield. Beeswax is Wickrow money."

Follow the scent into Wickrow at night. Baseline combat prowl (wisps, a
watch-dog); after encounter 2 the first **Press On** choice appears, odens
visible — pressing deeper reaches the loading dock where Ash finds a
guild-sealed candle stub *stamped with a delivery mark for Thimblefield* —
and no chandler delivers to witches on credit.

- District rule (Wickrow): *Chandlers' ward-lights everywhere — Moonlight
  costs +1, Guile −1 (bribes work here).*
- Reward: Gleam pouch + the candle stub (evidence 1/5). Unlocks the Magpie
  Exchange (Brindle appraises the stub — and goes quiet for half a second
  too long when he sees the seal. Players won't notice. Replayers will).
- **Bodkin enters here** (required — see [the-unraveler.md](../the-unraveler.md)):
  a scarred one-eyed grey tom who pulls Ash out of the fight's aftermath,
  teaches the charge trick ("watch the paws, not the claws"), and steers
  him toward Wick. **Hindsight rule: every Bodkin line must be literally
  true.** He never enters the parlor and never touches thread on-screen.

### L2 — "Creditors" *(2 prowls · heist + stealth)*

**Board card:** "The Chandlers' bank bought every debt Elspeth owed. Last
month. All at once. Banks are not sentimental."

Prowl A: case the counting-house by day (stealth, no-kill constraint — day-
side rules; Alarm here is *being noticed as a cat where cats aren't fed*).
Prowl B: the night heist — the ledger vault, guarded by wax-and-wick
constructs (candle-golems: snuffing them (Shadow) beats smashing them
(Ferocity) — taught by omens, not text).

The ledger (evidence 2/5): Wick's own hand — *buy Vane's paper, all of it;
call it due Lady's Day; she sells the parlor or the Court sells it for her.*
He wanted her **evicted**. You don't evict a woman you've had killed — an
alibi shaped like a confession, which is why nobody looks past it.

- Reward: heavy Gleam; **loadout editing unlocks** (Brindle: "You're going
  to want better pockets, pet.")

### L3 — "The Lamplighters Talk" *(2 prowls · choice-scenes)*

**Board card:** "The Lamplighters hate the Chandlers professionally. Hate is
chatty."

> **Module cut, approved 2026-08-03** (chapter1-build-plan.md Phase 0): the
> escort prowl and the Sway *meter* are deferred to Ch2+. Prowl A is a
> combat prowl through Chandler toughs on the shift-boss's round; the hall
> is a **choice-scene chain with a visible tally** — same beats, no new
> engine surface. The original escort/diplomacy design is preserved below
> for whenever the modules do get built.

*Original design (deferred):* find the Lamplighters' shift-boss (escort her
past Chandler toughs — escort intro, she walks her round *regardless*,
lighting lamps mid-combat: design showcase). Then the union hall
**diplomacy encounter** (Sway meter): cats are neutral parties; the slow
blink opens the final argument.

What they trade (evidence 3/5): Elspeth held the **Wickhouse ward** — the
great warehouse on the Mere-edge where the Guild keeps its wax... and
something older the wax was *bought to feed*. Wick stopped paying her two
years ago. **She kept sewing the ward anyway.** Free. Nobody sews free.

- Favor-knot economy unlocks (first knot owed to the Lamplighters — they'll
  call it in, Chapter 3).

### L4 — "The Ward That Failed" *(2 prowls · heist + ritual)*

**Board card:** "The Wickhouse ward is Elspeth's stitching. Go read her last
work. Bring nothing that burns."

> **Module cut, approved 2026-08-03:** the survival module is deferred to
> Ch2+. Prowl A becomes a **heist/Alarm** approach to the Wickhouse under a
> Mereside environment rule — the Drowned still arrive, but as a
> combat-with-environment prowl, not a hold-N-turns win condition.

Mereside fog. *Original design (deferred):* Prowl A: reach the Wickhouse past
the first **Drowned** (slow, patient, arriving — survival mission, hold 6
turns while the tide omens count down). Prowl B: the **ritual mission** — walk the ward-seam and
re-read the stitching (a pattern-matching encounter: reproduce her stitch
sequences with energy plays; combat-free, eerie, the game's first quiet
prowl since the parlor).

The reframe (evidence 4/5): the ward wasn't *broken*. It was **unpicked —
in her own stitch, backward**. Perfect technique, mirror-handed. Elspeth
taught exactly nobody. The Unpicked wasn't wearing unravelled thread as
clothing; it was wearing her *training*.

- The flashback system unlocks here if not already found via catnap:
  **Remembered Day 1 — "The Ninth Stray"** (how she found Ash: he was the
  ninth stray she tried that winter; the other eight declined the interview.
  Warm, funny, and — on replay — full of her stitching habits, which is
  evidence hiding as sentiment).

### L5 — "An Audience of Wax" *(1–2 prowls · boss + verdict)*

**Board card:** "Guildmaster Wick will see you now. Everyone in Wickrow is
suddenly very polite. Mind the big one."

The Guildhall. Boss: **the Tallowman** — Wick's bodyguard-construct, a
gentle-voiced tower of votive wax (phases: melts and reforms; the arena's
sconce-lights are snuffable to deny its healing — every mechanic taught this
chapter gets a use). Then Wick, small behind a big desk, talking fast.

His books say he was buying her *building*, not her silence: the Wickhouse
thing is dying and he's been feeding it wax and lies to keep the Guild's
monopoly on "safe dark." Her free ward-work was *ruining the terror margin*.
Squalid, cowardly, profitable — and not murder. Evidence 5/5, from his own
strongbox: a **delivery docket for the parlor, murder night, signed out —
and never delivered**. Someone used the Chandlers' cart-slot as a cover to
reach Needle Lane unremarked. Someone with guild paper, or good forgeries.

**The verdict (player choice, chapter ending):**
- **Expose him** — the Lamplighters get the streets, Wickrow's district rule
  permanently softens, big Favor payout; Wick becomes a bitter recurring
  informant.
- **Squeeze him** — a favor-knot on a Guildmaster (spendable later at
  terrible moments) + standing Gleam tribute; the Lamplighters remember that
  you didn't.

Either way, the chapter closes on the docket: the thin silver thread from
the prologue runs *toward Gravamen* — where the carts change hands. **Case
File II: cold storage.** (Paid unlock boundary lands here, at maximum
curiosity.)

## Design checks

- Every lead ends with a *thing* (evidence item with art), not a paragraph —
  the case is followable from the evidence board alone (owner low-text rule).
- Wick must stay charming-awful, never cartoon-evil: the chapter's thesis is
  that the city's ordinary greed *camouflaged* the real thing.
- L4's quiet prowl is the tone anchor — protect it from scope cuts.
- Difficulty: L5 beatable with either aspect and no side quests, but side
  quests bank Gleam/gear that make it comfortable (reward curiosity, never
  require it).
