# Monetization — DECIDED (2026-07-29; revised by owner 2026-08-31)

Owner decision (2026-08-31): **the game is free for now — everything built to
date, prologue through Chapter 1 — with an option to donate. Chapter 2 is
sold as a one-time purchase when it is ready.** This supersedes the
2026-07-29 model (free Chapter 1 → $6.99 "The Full Nine Lives" unlock),
which is retired as the launch plan; a complete-edition bundle may return
later once multiple paid chapters exist.

## The model

- **Free:** the entire current game — the murder, Chapter 1's investigation
  and verdict, all five minigames, all districts and builds shipped so far.
  **No ads, ever.** Replay value beyond the story comes from auto-generated
  cases (design doc still owed), clearly framed as practice/side content.
- **Donations — the tip jar:** an optional "support the developer" screen
  reachable from the Mantel/settings, using **consumable IAP tiers**
  (e.g. $1.99 / $4.99 / $9.99 with diegetic names — a saucer of milk, a fish
  supper, a whole ninth life). Zero gameplay effect, stated plainly. Store
  rules require tips to the developer to go through IAP on both platforms
  (this is developer support, not charity — charity donation rules do not
  apply). Consumables need no restore flow.
- **Chapter 2 — paid, when ready:** one non-consumable ($4.99–6.99, price
  decided at release), restorable, playable offline after purchase via
  cached entitlement. Later chapters likewise, or folded into a Complete
  Edition — decided then, not now.

## What this changes for shipping

- v1.0's store-side critical path shrinks: **no paywall, no entitlement
  gating, no restore-purchases flow at launch.** The only billing surface is
  the tip jar (consumables). If billing integration threatens the launch
  date, the tip jar may ship in the first update instead — owner call at
  build time.
- Small-business program enrollment (15% cut) still happens **before the
  first purchasable exists**, i.e. before the tip jar goes live.
- Store listings describe a free game with optional tips; no "IAP unlocks
  the full game" copy anywhere.

## Hard rules (violating any of these needs owner sign-off)

1. Single-player is **never** connectivity-gated. Purchases verify via cached
   store entitlements; the game must run on a plane forever.
2. No energy systems, no timers, no forced ads, no paid randomness (loot
   boxes are legally radioactive in BE/NL and the EU Digital Fairness Act is
   coming), no pay-to-win. Money buys deterministic content only. Tips buy
   nothing at all, and say so.
3. Never retro-monetize the campaign after launch (bait-and-switch =
   review-bomb). **Selling Chapter 2 does not violate this rule: it is new
   content. Anything that ever shipped free stays free forever.**
4. Enroll in **both** store small-business programs (15% cut) before the
   first purchasable ships. Art comes from **ChatGPT** (stills) and **Kling**
   (motion) — both on paid/commercial tiers only, with dated copies of each
   tool's commercial terms kept in `docs/publishing/`; AI music follows the
   same rule. Human-edit key assets; commission a human-made logo.

## Later phases (directional, not committed)

- **Post-launch:** Chapter 2 as the first paid drop; further case/class DLC
  at $2.99–3.99, deterministic; a Complete Edition bundle once ≥2 paid
  chapters exist; free content updates between paid drops. Possible Steam
  release at $9.99–12.99 (Steam pricing would bundle all chapters).
- The $2.99 Supporter Pack (cosmetics) is **cut** (owner-actions §6.4,
  resolved 2026-08-31) — the tip jar replaces its purpose without needing
  cosmetic art.
