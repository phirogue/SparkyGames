# Mechanics Mining — Tabletop, J-RPG, and Indie Systems

*Compiled 2026-07-30. Every mechanic judged against our actual system (energy
deck that never reshuffles, skill charges, telegraphed intents, the Approach,
3–5 minute runs) with adopt/adapt/reject verdicts.*

**Framing insight that drives most verdicts:** because our deck is typed
energy, "rolling a die" can be **"flip the top card of your deck"** — and
deck composition becomes the character sheet. A cat with lots of Guile in the
deck literally *is* good at Guile things. Every flip also burns fuel, so
every check costs time.

---

## 1. D&D 5e and derivatives

| Mechanic | Verdict | Our mapping |
|---|---|---|
| Advantage/Disadvantage | **ADOPT** | On any top-card flip: advantage = flip 2, either counts; disadvantage = flip 2, both must count. Flipped cards are spent — disadvantage burns the run timer twice as fast. Non-stacking, one icon. |
| Saving throws | **ADOPT** | "Resist intents": flip top card; matching type shrugs the condition. Ferocity resists force, Guile trickery, Shadow detection, Moonlight dread. Defense costs *time*. |
| Skill checks / DCs | **ADOPT** | Story obstacles = "flip N, need M of type X." One deck runs combat, story, and exploration — talking past the doorman leaves less fuel for the vault fight. |
| Short/long rests | **ADAPT** | We have long rests (loadout). Add the **Catnap**: restore charges/health by discarding top 3–5 cards unseen — recovery priced in fuel. Energy NEVER recovers. |
| Spell slots vs cantrips | **ADOPT** | Charges are slots. The missing half: an at-will basic action (our Scratch, plus maybe *Swipe*: 1 any-energy for small hit/guard) so no turn is dead. |
| Conditions | **ADAPT** | Cap at four, cat-flavored: *Pinned* (skill locked), *Spooked* (can't spend Ferocity), *Marked* (flips disadvantaged), *Soaked* (Shadow useless). Each cured by an obvious action. |
| Legendary actions | **ADOPT (bosses)** | **Boss Tempo**: every 3rd energy you spend, the boss takes a small telegraphed action between your plays. Greedy turns feed it. |
| Inspiration | **REJECT** | Second token economy; fold story rewards into advantage/energy grants. |

## 2. Modern narrative tabletop

| Mechanic | Verdict | Our mapping |
|---|---|---|
| Blades: position & effect | **ADAPT** | The Approach is proto-position. Add the *trade*: overcharge any skill (+1 energy) for greater effect, but the enemy's next intent upgrades. |
| Blades: **devil's bargains** | **ADOPT** | Choices that grant a real resource NOW for a *named, certain, deferred* complication logged in the Casebook and always collected. Curiosity-with-a-price = the most cat-detective mechanic possible. |
| Blades: flashbacks | **ADOPT (bounded)** | Once per adventure: pay energy retroactively to have *already prepared* (negate an intent, switch Approach, produce a clue). Whitelisted effects, cost 1–3 by implausibility. |
| Blades: stress | **ADAPT** | Reject the meter (we have two doom tracks already). Keep the push: **Overdraw** — draw extra now, permanently scuff an unseen card off the deck bottom. |
| PbtA success-at-cost | **ADOPT** | Energy wheel (Ferocity–Guile–Shadow–Moonlight): exact type = clean success; adjacent = success at a cost; opposed = fail forward, never a wall. |

## 3. J-RPGs

| Mechanic | Verdict | Our mapping |
|---|---|---|
| ATB/real-time | **REJECT** | Antithetical to a touch puzzle; its pressure function is absorbed by Boss Tempo. |
| Limit breaks | **ADAPT** | **Ninth Wind**: enemy hits fill a meter; full = next skill free. Rewards tanking the *right* hit. |
| Persona weakness/"One More" | **ADOPT (light)** | One hidden weakness per enemy. Matching-energy damage = **Expose**: cancel its telegraphed intent + refund 1 energy (once/enemy/turn). The **Case** approach reveals weaknesses — investigation becomes combat power. |
| Persona social links | **ADAPT (meta)** | **Informants** (pigeon fancier, rat king): deepened via quest outcomes and bargain fallout; grant free Cases, catnap spots, unique skills. Where bargain bills arrive. |
| Darkest Dungeon stress | **REJECT** | Needs long runs + parties. Keep *Spooked* + low-HP dread narration. |
| Chrono Trigger combos | **ADAPT** | **Harmony riders**: "if Shadowstep was used this turn, Pounce also silences." Loadout building = combo authorship. |
| FF job systems | **ADAPT (meta)** | **Nine lives as nine jobs**: each death moves Ash to the next life with a distinct passive/deck bias. Death gains texture, not just cost. |
| Pokémon type chart | **REJECT** | A 4×4 matrix triples decision load; single-weakness Expose delivers 90% at 20%. |

## 4. Recent indies

| Game | Verdict | The lesson for us |
|---|---|---|
| Cobalt Core | **ADAPT (prototype-gate)** | **Perches** (rafters/floor/shadows): some intents target a perch; Shadow repositions; dodging entirely becomes a fourth answer. Expensive — paper-test first. |
| Shogun Showdown | **ADAPT** | **Windup** keyword: pay now, release later for more — held charges vulnerable to Skill attacks. |
| Tactical Breach Wizards | **ADOPT** | Free undo within the turn until **Commit**; only flips/draws lock. UX, not rules — near-mandatory on mobile. |
| Blue Prince | **ADOPT** | **The Casebook**: knowledge-as-progression. Auto-records weaknesses, debts, secrets; some quests gate on *knowing*, not items. Even deaths pay evidence. |
| Balatro | **ADOPT/ADAPT** | Deck surgery at long rests (= leveling); **Charms** as rule-bending relics, hard-capped at ~3. |
| Little Kitty, Big City | **ADOPT (tone)** | The overworld is a consequence-free cat toybox; a few toy interactions quietly matter. Stakes live in encounters only. |
| Cult of the Lamb | **REJECT (scope)** | No second management game; the den stays a diegetic menu. |
| Slice & Dice | **ADOPT (principles)** | Single-digit numbers; deck type-counts always inspectable; **Sixth Sense** (once/fight: peek top 3, bottom one) as the luck valve. |

## 5. Ranked shortlist

1. **The Flip** — unified card-flip resolution (checks + saves + PbtA
   outcomes + advantage). Fuses resolution, stats, economy, and progression
   into one existing object. *The purest "simple rules, open doors" win.*
2. **Devil's Bargains** — temptation with a printed, certain, deferred price;
   the mechanical body of "decisions affect quests."
3. **Case → Expose** — deduce/reveal one weakness per enemy; matching-energy
   damage cancels its intent + refunds 1. Detective work = combat power.
4. **Boss Tempo** — legendary actions keyed to your energy spending; turn
   shape becomes a decision.
5. **Flashbacks** — once per adventure, pay energy retroactively to have
   prepared. The signature move of detectives and cats alike.

Honorable mentions: undo-until-Commit, Casebook, Catnap, Ninth Wind,
nine-lives-as-jobs. Full source list in the research transcript.

## Adoption sequencing (proposal)

- **Prologue/Ch1 (now):** undo-until-Commit; transparency principles.
- **Chapter 1 ships with:** The Flip for story checks + Devil's Bargains in
  quest events (both reuse existing nouns; huge payoff).
- **Chapter 2's "one new rule":** Case → Expose.
- **First boss redesign:** Boss Tempo (the Unpicked rematch).
- **Chapter 3+:** Flashbacks, Harmony riders, Windup, Ninth Wind.
- **Meta, when den ships:** Casebook, Informants, nine-lives-as-jobs, Charms.
