## Long description

Elspeth the seamstress-witch is dead in her chair, and the only witness is
her cat. Nobody hires a cat to solve a murder. Ash is on the case anyway.

**The Nine Lives of Ash** is a story-driven card game played in true
one-sitting runs — a prowl through the night city takes three to five
minutes, and every one of them moves the case.

- **You are the cat.** Stalk, ambush, loaf, purr, knock things off
  shelves. Every cat behavior is a real move with real rules.
- **A new kind of card fight.** Your skills are chosen before you go out;
  the deck is Ash himself — his ferocity, guile, shadow, and the witch's
  borrowed moonlight. When the deck runs dry, so has he.
- **A murder to solve.** Follow threads, press witnesses, mend wards,
  crack the counting-house — five kinds of casework beyond the fights,
  all feeding one investigation.
- **Death is not the end. It is a bureaucracy.** Lose a life and wake at
  the Hollow Court's desk, where a Clerk stamps the paperwork and the
  city remembers how you died.
- **Complete story, free.** The whole first case — no ads, no energy
  timers, nothing sold in your way. If you enjoy it, there is a tip jar.
  The second file is being written.
- **Fully offline.** The whole game works on a plane, forever.
