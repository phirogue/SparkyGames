The Nine Lives of Ash is made by SparkyGames. This policy describes what
information the game handles. The short version: **the game collects
nothing.**

## What we collect

Nothing. The Nine Lives of Ash is a fully offline, single-player game.

- **No account is created.** The game never asks who you are.
- **No analytics or tracking.** We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising
  identifiers, crash reporters, or telemetry of any kind.
- **No ads.** The game contains no advertising.
- **No network use.** The game does not connect to the internet to play.
  Your progress is saved only on your device.

## Your save data

Your game progress is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.
Deleting the app deletes your save. We cannot see it, back it up, or
recover it.

## Purchases

Optional purchases (such as tips to the developer or future story chapters)
are processed entirely by the app store you bought the game from — Apple's
App Store or Google Play. We receive no payment details. The stores'
own privacy policies govern those transactions:

- Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future update ever changes what the game handles (for example, adding
optional cloud saves), this policy will be updated first, and the change
will be called out in the update's store notes.
